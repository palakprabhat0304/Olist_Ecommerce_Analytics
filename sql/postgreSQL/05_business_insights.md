# Olist E-commerce Data Analysis
## Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset using PostgreSQL to understand customer behaviour, sales performance, delivery efficiency, customer satisfaction, and payment patterns.

### 2. Key Business Insights

#### Insight 1: Customer Retention
- Total unique customers: 96,096
- Repeat customers: 2,997
- Repeat customer rate: 3.12%

**Finding:** Only 3.12% of unique customers placed more than one order in the dataset.

**Recommendation:** Introduce customer loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted follow-up campaigns to encourage repeat purchases.

#### Insight 2: Customer Satisfaction
- Total reviews: 99,224
- Average review score: 4.09/5
- Positive reviews (4–5 stars): 76,470
- Negative reviews (1–2 stars): 14,575

**Finding:** Overall customer ratings are positive, but a considerable number of negative reviews indicate opportunities to improve customer experience.

**Recommendation:** Investigate recurring complaints and improve delivery communication, product quality, and customer support.

#### Insight 3: Delivery Performance
- Delivered orders with valid delivery dates: 96,470
- Late deliveries: 7,826
- Late delivery rate: 8.11%
- Average delay among late orders: 9.55 days

**Finding:** Although most delivered orders reached customers on time or early, late deliveries remain an important operational challenge.

**Recommendation:** Improve delivery-time estimation, logistics coordination, and proactive shipment tracking.

#### Insight 4: Delivery and Customer Satisfaction
- Average rating for late deliveries: 2.57/5
- Average rating for on-time/early deliveries: 4.29/5
- One-star review rate for late deliveries: 46.16%
- One-star review rate for on-time/early deliveries: 6.60%

**Finding:** Late deliveries are strongly associated with lower customer ratings and a higher proportion of one-star reviews.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize delayed shipments and notify customers proactively when delivery estimates change.

#### Insight 5: Payment Behaviour
- Credit card share of payment records: 73.92%
- Boleto share: 19.04%
- Voucher share: 5.56%

**Finding:** Credit cards represent the largest share of payment records in the dataset.

**Recommendation:** Maintain a smooth credit card payment experience while continuing to support alternative payment methods.

#### Insight 6: Average Order Value
- Total payment value: R$16,008,872.12
- Distinct orders: 99,440
- Average Order Value: R$160.99

**Finding:** The average order value provides a useful baseline for evaluating purchasing behaviour and sales performance.

**Recommendation:** Explore relevant product bundles, cross-selling, and personalized product recommendations to increase order value.

### 3. Overall Business Recommendations

1. Improve customer retention through loyalty initiatives.
2. Reduce delivery delays through better logistics planning.
3. Monitor customer reviews to identify recurring service issues.
4. Maintain reliable and convenient payment options.
5. Explore opportunities to increase average order value.
6. Track customer satisfaction and delivery performance regularly.

### 4. Conclusion

The analysis highlights important opportunities in customer retention, delivery operations, customer satisfaction, and payment behaviour. Delivery performance stands out as a key area for improvement because late deliveries are associated with substantially lower review scores.

The findings can help the business prioritize operational improvements and make more informed, data-driven decisions.

*Note: Findings are descriptive and based on the available dataset. Associations do not establish causation. Monetary values are in Brazilian Real (BRL).*
