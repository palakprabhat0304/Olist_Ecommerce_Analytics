# Portfolio Notes

## Business questions
1. How is revenue changing over time?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. Which customer states have higher/lower AOV?
4. Which customer segments deserve retention investment?
5. How well do customers retain after their first purchase?

## Recommended business recommendations
Recommendations should be written only after running the analysis. Avoid inventing findings. Use the SQL outputs to identify:
- high-value Champions for loyalty programs;
- At Risk customers for reactivation;
- Lost customers for win-back campaigns;
- weak-performing states for localized marketing;
- high-revenue categories for merchandising and cross-sell opportunities.
