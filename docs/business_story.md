# Business Story Framework

Use the actual SQL outputs to replace the placeholders below; do not invent findings.

1. **Customer concentration:** `[X%]` of customers are in Champions/Loyal segments and contribute `[Y%]` of revenue.
2. **Retention gap:** The selected cohort retains `[X%]` after month 1 and `[Y%]` after month 3, showing the strength/weakness of repeat purchasing.
3. **Risk pool:** At Risk + Lost customers represent `[X%]` of customers and `[Y%]` of revenue, defining the size of the reactivation opportunity.
4. **Geographic/category concentration:** Top states/categories contribute `[X%]` of revenue, identifying where growth is concentrated.

Recommendations should directly reference these measured values.
